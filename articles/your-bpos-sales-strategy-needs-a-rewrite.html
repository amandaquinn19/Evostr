How to Apply This Now

Identify your niche. The more specialized you are, the easier it is to stand out.

Shift from chasing buyers to positioning as the authority. Start educating your market instead of just pitching.

Create content that builds trust before the sales conversation. Decision-makers pay a premium for BPOs that shape the conversation.

FAQ: Building a Demand Generation Motion

We don't have a marketing function. Where do we even start?

Start with whoever's already closest to the market — a founder, a head of sales, whoever's been fielding the 'what do you actually do' question in every pitch. One consistent voice publishing regularly beats a marketing team producing generic content nobody reads.

Do we stop chasing RFPs and referrals while we build this out?

No — keep responding to what's already in front of you. Demand generation is additive and takes months to compound, so the existing pipeline keeps the lights on while it builds.

How long before this actually shows up in the pipeline?

Expect a couple of quarters before you see inbound conversations that reference your content specifically. It compounds — the ninetieth post works better than the tenth because the backlog of trust is bigger, not because any single piece went viral.
